## 📌 Project Overview
The **Campus Event & Student Club Hub** is a centralized, web-based platform designed to streamline student organization activities, enhance campus engagement, and automate administrative workflows across Zewail City. Built following the strict **Model-View-Controller (MVC)** architectural pattern, the platform bridges the gap between students, student clubs, and university administrators.

### Core Features:
- **Event Management & Discovery:** Interactive browsing and creation of campus events with venue and capacity controls.
- **Registration & Digital Ticketing:** Instant registration with auto-generated, verifiable QR code digital tickets.
- **On-Site Attendance Tracking:** Mobile-responsive QR code scanner for club organizers to check in attendees seamlessly.
- **Budget & Expense Management:** Comprehensive financial ledger for tracking club allocations, submitting audited expenses with receipt uploads, and real-time balance calculations.
- **Personalized Dashboards:** Dedicated operational views tailored for Standard Students, Club Leaders, and System Administrators (Student Affairs).
